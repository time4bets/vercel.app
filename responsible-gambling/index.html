Responsible Gambling

Keep gambling recreational with clear limits, realistic expectations, regular breaks and protection for minors.

Last updated: May 2026

Entertainment only

Casino games and sports betting should be treated as paid entertainment, not income, investment or a way to solve financial stress.

Set limits before play

Decide in advance how much money and time you can afford. Stop when the limit is reached, even if the session feels unfinished.

  • Set deposit limits if available
  • Take breaks during long sessions
  • Never chase losses

Warning signs

Pause or seek help if gambling affects sleep, work, relationships, bills, mood or the ability to stop.

Support tools

Cooling-off, self-exclusion and account limits can help when gambling becomes difficult to control. These tools should be used early, not only after serious harm.

Protect minors

Do not allow minors to access gambling accounts, payment details or devices with saved login sessions.
