Registration FAQ

The answers have been rewritten for this new account page.

Why should details be accurate?
Incorrect details can create problems during verification or withdrawal.
Do I have to take a bonus?
No. A bonus should be accepted only if its conditions are suitable.
Can I create more than one account?
Duplicate accounts are usually restricted and can cause account issues.
When can verification happen?
It can happen during account checks, payment review or withdrawal processing.
Should I register on a shared device?
It is safer to use a private device and log out after each session.
