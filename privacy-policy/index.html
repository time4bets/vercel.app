Privacy Policy

Read how account, technical and payment-related information may be handled when using a gambling platform.

Last updated: May 2026

Purpose of this policy

This rewritten policy page explains privacy topics in a clearer order. It is intended to help visitors understand what types of information may be connected with account access, security, payments and website performance.

Account information

Registration or support activity may involve contact details, age confirmation, account identifiers and communication history. Users should keep this information accurate and protected.

  • Do not share login credentials
  • Keep contact details current
  • Use a private email address where possible

Technical information

The website may rely on browser, device, session and security data to keep pages usable, detect technical issues and maintain basic protection against abuse.

Payment records

Cashier activity can create records linked to deposits, withdrawals, method availability, verification requests, transaction status and bonus conditions.

User choices

Visitors can control some privacy settings through their browser, device, account tools and communication preferences. Shared devices should be avoided for gambling accounts.
